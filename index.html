National Helpline 109

Ministry of Women & Children Affairs

24/7 national helpline for violence against women and children. Connects callers to police, local administration, and NGOs simultaneously. Available everywhere in Bangladesh.

🕐 24 hours / 7 days
📞 Call 109

National emergency police line for immediate safety threats, violence, or crimes in progress. Available 24/7 across the country.

🕐 24 hours / 7 days
📞 Call 999

What to Do in an Emergency

১

If you are in immediate danger, call Police 999 or National Helpline 109 first.

২

If you need a safe place to stay, contact BNWLA or ASK for emergency shelter.

৩

For free legal help, call BLAST (01715-220220) or the National Legal Aid Hotline 16357.

৪

For medical care after violence, go to the nearest OCC (hospital) or call 109 to be connected.
